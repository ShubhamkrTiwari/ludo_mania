**Developer:** Shubham Kr Tiwari  
**Effective Date:** October 24, 2023  
**Last Updated:** October 24, 2023

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Ludo Mania** developed by **Shubham Kr Tiwari** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application **Ludo Mania** (Package Name: `com.ludomania.game`) handles your information.

Ludo Mania is an offline/local multi-player board game providing Classic 4-Player Avatar Ludo, Snake & Ladders, and Funny Party Race.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
**Ludo Mania DOES NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) or user data to external servers.**

### A. Local Storage Only
All game settings, player custom avatar names, local game progress, sound preferences, and win leaderboard statistics are saved **strictly locally on your device** using local storage (`SharedPreferences` / system cache). This data never leaves your device.

### B. Device Permissions Used
- **MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS**: Required to play local game audio sound effects (dice rolling, hopping, snake bites, victory chimes) through the device speaker.
- **VIBRATE**: Required for optional in-game haptic feedback effects.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services
Ludo Mania does not integrate third-party tracking SDKs, analytics frameworks, or external ad networks that collect or track user data.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy
Our game **Ludo Mania** is designed for a general audience and is family-friendly (rated PEGI 3 / Everyone). We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children under the age of 13.

---

## 5. Security
Because all your game data is stored exclusively on your local device, your privacy and data security are safeguarded by your own device's security system. Uninstalling the app will clear locally stored game preferences.
